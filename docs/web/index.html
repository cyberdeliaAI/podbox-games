PodBox games

Games for the Arcade of PodBox, the firmware for the iPod Video and iPod Classic. Copy a .pbg to a folder called Games on the iPod, eject it and pick Arcade on the main menu. Five of them play in a browser too: the games that follow your music can't.

Pocket Wings 3D

A relaxed flight over six 3D landscapes. Free Flight, or Discovery: thirty landmarks to find.
